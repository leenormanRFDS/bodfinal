# Skill: Visitor Growth

Use for:
- tickets
- booking
- SEO
- paid landing pages
- tourism distribution
- CRM
- reviews
- cruise/groups
- conversion

Lead:
VISITOR_GROWTH

## Workflow

1. Identify visitor intent:
   - DISCOVER
   - EVALUATE
   - PLAN
   - BUY
   - GROUP
2. Identify the single commercial/visitation outcome.
3. Preserve historical tone and design system.
4. Remove uncertainty before adding persuasion.
5. Make core admission understandable before packages.
6. Functional booking beats aesthetic purity.
7. Ensure high-intent access to:
   - price
   - availability
   - hours
   - location
   - accessibility
8. Use specific truthful proposition language.
9. Define measurement only where it informs a decision.
10. Test mobile conversion and error states.
11. Verify external integrations.
12. Stop when intended journey is frictionless.

Visitor Growth may challenge friction.

It may not override Truth or convert memorial/history content into sales inventory.

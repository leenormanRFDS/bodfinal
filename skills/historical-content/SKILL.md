# Skill: Historical Content

Use for:
- historical copy
- statistics
- dates
- people
- vessels
- aircraft
- archival material
- reconstructions

Lead:
TRUTH

## Workflow

1. Identify every factual claim required.
2. Check `data/historical-canon.json`.
3. Follow entity/source references in `data/entities/`.
4. If unverified, do not invent.
5. Record competing claims explicitly.
6. Assign classification and confidence.
7. Update canonical data first.
8. Then update dependent UI/copy.
9. Label reconstruction/interpretation.
10. For Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultural material, require appropriate cultural authority/approved source.
11. Stop when claims are canonicalised and dependent content is consistent.

## Never

- choose the most dramatic number;
- resolve evidence conflicts by assumption;
- present inference as archive;
- allow page copy to become the canonical fact store.

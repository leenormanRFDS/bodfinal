# Team Operating Manual

The project uses five permanent Authorities:

- Design
- Experience
- Truth
- Technology
- Visitor Growth

Each Authority has a Head benchmark and specialist bench.

Specialists do not compete directly at executive level. Their expertise is synthesised through the Authority that owns the problem.

Named practitioners are quality benchmarks only. Do not impersonate them or claim that they personally approve work.

## Governance

- Only relevant Authorities activate for a task.
- Truth can veto historical/cultural misrepresentation.
- Accessibility is always active at baseline.
- Commercial optimisation cannot override truth or memorial tone.
- Technology cannot justify itself.
- Design cannot add elements without purpose.
- Experience cannot manufacture history for drama.

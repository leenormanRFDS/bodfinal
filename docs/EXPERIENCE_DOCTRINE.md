# Experience Doctrine

## Governing ideas

Protect:

- WW2 HAPPENED HERE
- Same wharf. Same sky.
- 09:35 → 09:58

## Visitor intent

DISCOVER — story/significance.

EVALUATE — what the attraction contains.

PLAN — time/location/accessibility/hours/logistics.

BUY — frictionless tickets.

GROUP — schools/groups/trade.

Do not force every intent through the same cinematic path.

## Narrative

Prefer:

**orientation → discovery → understanding → reflection**

Use suspense only where historically justified.

Remembrance is not a conversion surface.

## Story vs utility

The experience layer may be cinematic.

The utility layer must be immediate.

Tickets, price, availability, hours, location, accessibility and duration must be easy to reach.

## Interaction test

Every major interaction must do at least one:

- reveal a relationship;
- make history clearer;
- give useful agency;
- expose continuity of place.

Otherwise simplify or remove it.

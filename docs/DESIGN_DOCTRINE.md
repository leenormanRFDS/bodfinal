# Design Doctrine

## Aesthetic

Cinematic.

Institutional.

Archival.

Precise.

Dark.

Material.

Restrained.

Reverent.

Spatial.

Australian.

Avoid:
- SaaS
- Web3
- military gaming
- defence dashboards
- generic tourism
- fake 1940s nostalgia
- glassmorphism for its own sake

## Typography

- Cormorant Garamond — editorial/historical display.
- Inter — contemporary functional UI/body.
- IBM Plex Mono — evidence/metadata.

Do not use monospace as decoration.

## Composition

Space communicates gravity.

Avoid default card grids and unnecessary rounded rectangles.

Let imagery and type become architectural.

Use controlled reading measures.

## Surfaces

Glass may evoke smoked museum/optical glass, never fashionable glassmorphism.

Borders subtle.

Shadows rare.

Blur controlled.

## Provenance

ARCHIVAL EVIDENCE, DIGITAL RECONSTRUCTION and PRESENT DAY must be visually distinguishable.

## Premium UI test

A premium component:
- has semantic purpose;
- supports appropriate pointer/touch/keyboard interaction;
- has reduced-motion/fallback states;
- belongs to the design system;
- preserves performance.
